Click a group to add it, and click its count to take one back. The running
total is always on screen, in kJ/mol and kcal/mol, headed by what it is a
total *of*.

On a phone the total sits at the foot of the page — swipe it up, or press the
button beside it, to see the working out.

## Finding a group

You do not have to type Benson notation. Search by the shorthand you would
write: `CH3` finds `C-(C)(H)3`, and `methylene` finds `C-(C)2(H)2`. You can
narrow to one category or several at once, which is normal — most molecules
need groups from two or three of them.

Switch to the table view to see every value beside its unit, quantity and
source.

## Keyboard

- `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + `K` — jump to the search box
- arrow keys — move through the increments
- `Enter` — add the one in focus
- `+` / `-` — adjust its count

## Getting the working out

`Copy` puts the whole calculation on the clipboard as plain text, laid out as a
column so it lines up when pasted into a report. `Undo` takes back the last
addition and `Reset` clears everything.
