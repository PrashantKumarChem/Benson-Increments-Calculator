The Benson Increments Calculator estimates the standard enthalpy of formation
of an organic molecule from Benson group increments. You choose the groups and
corrections your molecule needs; it does the arithmetic and shows the working.

It is deliberately not a structure parser. The calculator does not inspect a
molecule or decide which groups it requires, because deciding that is the skill
being taught. The reasoning stays with you and the arithmetic stays visible.

Built for Chemistry C450/C540 at Indiana University Bloomington by Prashant
Kumar and Nicola L. B. Pohl. The increment values follow Cohen and Benson
(1993); the full citation, and everything else the numbers rest on, is in
Sources and References at the foot of this page.

This page is the whole of the documentation. If something here is wrong or
unclear, it is a file in `reference/` and it can be fixed without touching any
code — see the last section.
