# Fall 2025 Teaching Artifacts (Year 2)

**Course:** CHEM-C 450 / CHEM-C 540 (Advanced Organic Chemistry), Indiana University Bloomington  
**Instructors:** Prashant Kumar, Dr. Nicola L. B. Pohl  

## Included Files

1. **`Google_Colab_Exercise_Benson_Increments_Calculator.pdf`**  
   Classroom exercise guide for students providing step-by-step instructions to download the notebook and CSV from Canvas, log into Google Colab with their university credentials, upload files, and execute calculations without local environment dependencies.

2. **`Benson_Increments_Calculator_Colab_2025.ipynb`**  
   The updated Colab-compatible Jupyter notebook.

3. **`increment_correction_table_2025.csv`**  
   Expanded CSV dataset incorporating cyclohexane $A$-values alongside CH, CHO, and ring corrections.
